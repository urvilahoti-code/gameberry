EPFO clears monthly payout option for retiring PF members

The EPFO board has approved a voluntary systematic withdrawal plan, letting retiring members take their PF corpus in monthly, quarterly or half-yearly instalments instead of one lump sum. The minimum balance members must keep drops from 25% to 10%. The change still needs government notification before it takes effect.
